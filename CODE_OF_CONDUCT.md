# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Expected behaviour:

- Be respectful and patient with people of every experience level.
- Give and accept critical feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the issue tracker, pull
requests, discussions, and any other place where someone represents the
project in public.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private
reporting form:
<https://github.com/vbcherepanov/total-agent-memory/security/advisories/new>
(the same private channel that [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) uses). Reports are
handled confidentially.
